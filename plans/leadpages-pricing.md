# Leadpages Pricing

Last updated: 2026-07-10

> Machine-readable pricing reference for the plans sold at leadpages.com. All prices in USD.

## Plans

All prices in USD. No traffic caps on any plan. There is no free plan: every paid plan includes a 7-day free trial (credit card required), and billing starts when the trial ends unless you cancel.

Plans come in two lines, both sold on leadpages.com/pricing:

- Publishing plans (Starter, Pro, Business) publish pages, sites, and blogs. Every plan
  includes a set number of sites (Starter 1 · Pro 1 · Business 2 · Grow 4 · Optimize 6 · Scale 10).
- Optimization plans (Grow, Optimize, Scale) add A/B testing, Smart Traffic, personalization, and lead intelligence.

## Comparison Notes

- **No traffic caps**: Unlike Unbounce ($149/mo, 20K visitor cap) and Instapage ($199/mo, 15K visitor cap), Leadpages has no traffic caps on any plan.
- **A/B testing from $99**: Unbounce gates A/B testing to $149+. Instapage gates A/B testing to $199+. HubSpot Marketing Hub requires $890/mo for page-level A/B testing.
- **All-in-one**: The optimization plans replace separate tools for page building, A/B testing, heatmaps, lead enrichment, and personalization.

## FAQ

### How does billing work?
Plans are billed monthly, or annually at 20% off the monthly rate. All prices are in USD.

### Is there a free plan?
No. There is a 7-day free trial on every paid plan; a credit card is required to start it, and billing begins when the trial ends unless you cancel.

### Can I change plans later?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account billing settings.
